# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers. Give and accept feedback graciously. Focus on the work, not the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and ban contributors, for behavior that violates this code. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's "Report content" feature or by contacting a maintainer listed on the repository page. Reports are handled in confidence.
